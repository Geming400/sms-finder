# Sms Finder

This is a simple app made to find your phone trough sms.
This can be useful if you don't use Google's services or if you don't often enable your mobile data.

I will work on this later.

## Google Play ?

No. There are multiple reasons:
- I'm not giving google my info to upload a silly app
- I'm not giving them money (https://keepandroidopen.org/)
- https://github.com/XomaDev/MIUI-autostart#google-play-policy
- Like I said it's just a silly app
